# Wendy Foils research notes (2026-07-03)

Backing research for the tuned logic in CLAUDE.md section 3. Three parallel research passes: spots, forecast sources, beginner skill/conditions.

## Skill level & conditions (beginner winger, surf background, 5m / 95L)

- 5m at ~75-90kg: foils from ~13kt, ideal 15-20kt (sweet spot 16-18), beginner ceiling ~22kt, hard safety ceiling ~23kt. Above that a rider drops to a 4m; a beginner just doesn't go.
- 95L board: beginner rule is bodyweight + ~30L (~1.3x). 95L is slightly sporty for this rider but fine *because* he's a balance-confident surfer. Lower volume raises the practical minimum wind — lean to 15+kt rather than counting on 13kt days. Doesn't change the upper limit.
- Steady > strong: "consistent 15kt beats gusty 20kt." Target gusts within ~25% of avg; gust − avg ≤ ~5kt is clean.
- Direction is the #1 safety variable: side/side-onshore best, straight onshore usable-but-annoying, **offshore = hard no-go** (failures carry you out). Inland lakes safer than open coast.
- Surf background: compresses timeline (athletic learners ~1wk to foil, ~3wk to ride upwind) but wing handling + fore-aft foil pitch are new. Flat water first; small clean waves become an asset only once foil-stable — treat waves as a negative in phase 1.

## Forecast sources

- **Wind gold standard for NL: KNMI HARMONIE-AROME** (~2km, ~2.5-day horizon), best for sea breeze. Get it keyless via Open-Meteo `models=knmi_harmonie_arome_netherlands`. Backups: `icon_eu`, `best_match` (blends ECMWF to ~15 days) for the week-ahead view; ECMWF for medium range.
- **Waves (coast only): Open-Meteo Marine API `models=ewam`** (DWD EWAM ~5km). Inland lakes = no swell, skip the marine call. Coastal accuracy limited (no surf-zone/tide resolution) — use as a "meaningful swell?" flag.
- **Windguru** = best human cross-check (shows GFS/ICON/AROME/HARMONIE side by side) but no clean API — link, don't scrape. IJsselmeer N `windguru.cz/19`, S `windguru.cz/3601`, Wijk aan Zee `windguru.cz/113`.
- Open-Meteo: no key, CC BY 4.0, non-commercial free tier 10k calls/day. KNMI direction may be off up to 15° without projection correction.

Sources: open-meteo.com (KNMI, marine, main docs, pricing/terms), community.windy.com (HARMONIE thread), english.knmidata.nl, surfschoolcastricum.com, windguru.cz.
