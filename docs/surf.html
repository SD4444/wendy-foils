How the call is made

∿

Size

Face height 1.0 m+, waist-high and up. Sweet spot 1.4–2.4 m (chest to a bit overhead). Above ~3.2 m the open beaches get heavy; sheltered spots hold. Same-day size is corrected against the live buoys.

≈

Period

Under 6.5 s is windswell mush and gets marked down. 10 s+ is proper groundswell and scores up.

➚

Wind

Glassy or offshore (E) is best. Onshore 14 kt+ is blown out and the hour is thrown away.

☼

Tide & light

Daylight only. Each spot has its own tide preference (Côte des Basques low, Gravière incoming, beach breaks mid). Times are model-interpolated; SHOM has the official ones.
