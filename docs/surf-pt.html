How spots are scored

∿

Size

Wave face at least 1.0 m (waist-high). Best between 1.4 and 2.4 m. Above 3.2 m the open beaches close out and the sheltered spots score higher. Today's heights are adjusted to the live buoys.

≈

Period

Under 6.5 s is short, weak windswell and scores lower. 10 s or more is groundswell and scores higher.

➚

Wind

Glassy or offshore (east) is best. Onshore at 14 kt or more counts as unsurfable.

☼

Tide and daylight

Daylight hours only. Each spot has a preferred tide (Côte des Basques low, Gravière incoming, most beach breaks mid). Tide times come from a model and can be up to an hour off; the SHOM link has the official table.
