How the call is made

◓

Foilable wind

13–22 kt, ideal 15–20. Below 13 you can't get up on the 95L.

≈

Steady, not spiky

Gust minus average ≤ 5 kt. A 15-gusting-28 day is a no-go on a 5m.

➚

Direction

Side-shore / side-onshore only. Offshore blocks the spot regardless of speed.
